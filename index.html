Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective 25 September 2026

Untraceable AI runs a language model on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server belonging to us. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data — we have nowhere to put it. The app contains no third-party AI service: your prompts, documents and photos are processed by the model bundled inside the app, on your device, and are never sent to any AI provider.

The short version. Your conversations, documents and photos are processed on the device and written only to the app's own storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere unless you turn on web search, open the in-app browser, or use dictation — each of which is described below, in plain terms. The app contains no cloud AI service of any kind. Before the first transmission to a search engine, the app shows you a permission screen naming the recipient and listing exactly what would be sent, and nothing is sent unless you agree.

Those files are also included in unencrypted iTunes/Finder backups and, if you have it enabled, in iCloud device backups. Use a device passcode, and encrypt local backups.

Permission before anything is sent

Web search is optional and switched off until you enable it. The first time it would actually transmit something, the app shows a full-screen disclosure that states:

  • who receives the data — the company name and the exact server host, for example duckduckgo.com;
  • every category of data that will be sent, itemised;
  • what will not be sent;
  • that the recipient handles it under their own privacy policy, not ours.

You then choose to agree or to cancel. Cancelling leaves the message unsent and the permission ungranted, and the app makes no request. Permission is recorded separately for each recipient — agreeing to one provider says nothing about any other. You can withdraw every permission at any time in Settings → Data sharing, or by switching web search off again; the disclosure then appears the next time.

We only integrate third-party services that publish a privacy policy offering protection equal to or stronger than this one, and we never route your data through any server of ours — there isn't one. The complete list of recipients is below.

What leaves the device, and only when you ask

1. Web search — off by default

Tapping the globe in the composer asks your permission the first time, then turns on web search for that conversation. When it is on, the text of your query is sent directly from your phone to public search engines (DuckDuckGo, Bing and Google, depending on the mode) so that results can be fetched. Result images and video thumbnails are loaded from the sites that host them, including YouTube. We are not a party to those requests and never see them; they are governed by each service's own privacy policy. Turn web search off and no such request is made.

The "Location" search mode is a search keyword only. The app does not use Core Location, has no location permission and never reads your GPS position.

2. The in-app browser

Opening a link from a search result loads that page in an in-app browser, which behaves like any browser: the site you visit sees your request. The page assistant also fetches the site's small icon from Google's public favicon service, which means that request tells Google the domain you are viewing. Nothing else about the page, and nothing you type in the assistant, is sent there.

3. Dictation

The microphone button uses Apple's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device, language and settings, iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers to transcribe it, and Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing. We never receive the audio or the transcript. If you prefer nothing to leave the device, type instead of dictating.

  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers or SDKs.
  • No accounts, no email collection, no contact list access.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. A language model can produce inaccurate or unsuitable text, and the web search feature reaches the open internet, so younger users should be supervised.

Keeping and deleting your data

Conversations stay on your device until you delete them. Remove a single session from the "sessions" sheet, or delete the app to remove everything it ever stored, including any model you imported. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete, export or restore on your behalf — and no request you could make to us would reach your conversations.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data held by a company. We hold none: the app performs no collection, and the developer operates no server that receives app data. Your data is under your control on your own device.
