Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 20 September 2026

Untracable AI runs a language model on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server belonging to us. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data — we have nowhere to put it.

The short version. Your conversations, documents and photos are processed on the device and written only to the app's own storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere unless you turn on web search, open the in-app browser, connect your own cloud AI key, or use dictation — each of which is described below, in plain terms.

Who we are

Untracable AI is published by an independent developer. Contact: sivavimelrajhen@gmail.com. This policy covers the iOS app "Untracable AI" (bundle identifier com.svr.untraceableai) and nothing else.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored locally, in the app's own container, and is never sent to us:

ItemWhere it lives
Chat messagesThe app's Documents folder, as plain JSON files (the last five sessions)
Attached photosHeld in memory for the turn; the extracted description is stored with the message
Attached documentsText is extracted on device; the excerpt used for the answer is stored with the message
The language modelBundled inside the app; any model you import yourself goes to the app's Documents folder
Your settingsThe app's preferences (theme, accent, system instruction)
Cloud API keysThe app's preferences, if and only if you add one
Two things worth knowing about local storage. The app's Documents folder is visible in the iOS Files app under "On My iPhone", so anyone who can unlock your phone can read your saved conversations. Those files are also included in unencrypted iTunes/Finder backups and, if you have it enabled, in iCloud device backups. Use a device passcode, and encrypt local backups.

What leaves the device, and only when you ask

1. Web search — off by default

Tapping the globe in the composer turns on web search for that conversation. When it is on, the text of your query is sent directly from your phone to public search engines (DuckDuckGo, Bing and Google, depending on the mode) so that results can be fetched. Result images and video thumbnails are loaded from the sites that host them, including YouTube. We are not a party to those requests and never see them; they are governed by each service's own privacy policy. Turn web search off and no such request is made.

The "Location" search mode is a search keyword only. The app does not use Core Location, has no location permission and never reads your GPS position.

2. The in-app browser

Opening a link from a search result loads that page in an in-app browser, which behaves like any browser: the site you visit sees your request. The page assistant also fetches the site's small icon from Google's public favicon service, which means that request tells Google the domain you are viewing. Nothing else about the page, and nothing you type in the assistant, is sent there.

3. Your own cloud AI key — not configured out of the box

You may optionally connect an account you already have with Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, or any custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. If you do, prompts and any attachment you send on that turn go from your phone straight to that provider, under their terms and their privacy policy. The key is stored on your device and is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Delete the key in the app and the app stops contacting them. With no key configured — the default — the app never talks to any AI provider, because the model is already on your phone.

4. Dictation

The microphone button uses Apple's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device, language and settings, iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers to transcribe it, and Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing. We never receive the audio or the transcript. If you prefer nothing to leave the device, type instead of dictating.

What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics of any kind.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers or SDKs.
  • No accounts, no email collection, no contact list access.
  • No location access.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — we hold none to sell or share.

Permissions the app may ask for

PermissionWhy
MicrophoneOnly while you hold dictation, to capture speech for transcription
Speech recognitionTo turn that audio into text, using Apple's recogniser
CameraOnly when you choose "camera" in Add sources, to attach a photo for on-device analysis

Each is requested at the moment you first use that feature, and declining it disables only that feature. Photo and document analysis itself runs on the device using Apple's Vision framework — the image is not uploaded anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. A language model can produce inaccurate or unsuitable text, and the web search feature reaches the open internet, so younger users should be supervised.

Keeping and deleting your data

Conversations stay on your device until you delete them. Remove a single session from the "sessions" sheet, or delete the app to remove everything it ever stored, including any API keys and imported models. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete, export or restore on your behalf — and no request you could make to us would reach your conversations.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data held by a company. We hold none: the app performs no collection, and the developer operates no server that receives app data. Your data is under your control on your own device.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this page is updated and the version and date at the top change with it. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
